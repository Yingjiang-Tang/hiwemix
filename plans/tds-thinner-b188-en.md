# HAIWEN Thinner Series — B-188 High Performance Thinner

## 1. Product Overview

HAIWEN C-166 / B-188 / A-199 Thinner Series is specially developed for HAIWEN automotive refinish systems. The series effectively reduces spray viscosity, improves sprayability and leveling, and ensures stable film formation under different application conditions.

## 2. Product Characteristics

### B-188 High Performance Thinner

- Higher solvency power
- Improved atomization and flow
- Especially recommended for primer and high-build applications

## 3. Thinner Selection Guide

Select the appropriate thinner according to ambient temperature:

| Ambient Temperature | Recommended Thinner          |
|---------------------|------------------------------|
| 0°C – 15°C          | Fast Drying Type (Fast)      |
| 15°C – 25°C         | Standard Drying Type (Normal)|
| 25°C – 35°C         | Slow Drying Type (Slow)      |

## 4. Notes

- B-188 has stronger dissolving capacity than C-166
- Select thinner grade according to ambient temperature and product system
- Seal container tightly after use to prevent solvent volatilization
- Avoid excessive thinning which may affect film performance

## 5. Safety Information

- For professional use only
- Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Flammable: keep away from heat, sparks, and open flames
- Keep out of reach of children
