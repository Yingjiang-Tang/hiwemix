# HAIWEN TGM-606 Anti-Blushing Agent

## 1. Application & Features

### Prevents Blushing
Eliminates paint film blushing, moisture entrapment, and hazing caused by low temperatures (near the dew point), high humidity, or moisture in the compressed air.

### Enhances Performance
Slows down solvent evaporation to minimize moisture condensation, while improving paint leveling and gloss.

### Working Principle
Introduces high-boiling point solvents into the system to retard evaporation.

## 2. Dosage

Mix thoroughly into the paint system by replacing **10% to 15% of the thinner volume with TGM-606** before application.

## 3. Notes

- For 2K paints and clear coats
- Use when ambient humidity is high or temperature is near the dew point
- Replacing part of the thinner (not adding extra on top)
- Mix thoroughly to ensure uniform distribution
- Shelf life and storage: refer to standard HAIWEN additive storage conditions (cool, dry, well-ventilated place)
