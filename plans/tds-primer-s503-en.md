# HAIWEN S-503 1K White Primer

## Product Overview

HAIWEN S-503 1K White Primer is suitable for general automotive plastic substrates. It provides excellent adhesion, easy sanding, and uniform coverage. Mixing Ratio: **1 : 0.8–1 (Primer : Thinner)**, Recommended Thinner: **C-166**.

## Key Advantages

- Single-component, easy to spray
- Good adhesion for standard plastic substrates
- Smooth application and easy sanding
- White color suitable for various applications

## Recommended Thinner

- C-166 Series

## Suitable Substrates

- General automotive plastics (ABS, TPO, etc.)

## Topcoat

- 2K Polyurethane, Acrylic, or Clear Coat

## Drying Time

| Temperature | 25 °C |
|---|---|
| Viscosity | 15–18 s |
| Touch Dry | 10–15 min |
| Sandable | 20 min |

## Mixing & Applications

- **Mixing Ratio (Volume):** 1 : 1 (Primer : Thinner)
- **Application:** 1–2 coats, 30–50 μm
- **Coverage:** 8–10 m²/L
- **Shelf Life:** 12 months

## Notes

- Stir well before use
- Close container after use to prevent solvent loss
- Avoid overly thick application
- Compatible with 1K/2K topcoats
- Ideal for bumpers, trims, and plastic panels

## Safety

- For professional use only
- Wear PPE
- Keep away from heat, sparks, and children

## Packing & Storage

- 1 L / Tin, 4 L / Tin
- **Shelf life:** 12 months unopened, store in a cool, ventilated, dry place
