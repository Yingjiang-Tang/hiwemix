# HAIWEN S-508 2K Grey Filler

## Product Overview

HAIWEN S-508 2K Grey Filler is a professional-grade surface preparation primer designed for both automotive refinishing and furniture painting applications. This high-build grey filler effectively fills minor imperfections, scratches, and surface irregularities, creating a smooth uniform base for subsequent topcoat application. Its versatile formulation makes it the ideal choice for workshops handling both car body repair and furniture finishing projects.

We also have two other colors: a black version (model **S-515**) and a white version (model **S-514**).

## Key Features

- **Versatile 2K Grey Filler** — Suitable for both automotive surface preparation and furniture painting applications
- **High-Build Formulation** — Fills minor imperfections and provides smooth sanding surface for professional topcoat results
- **Easy 4:1 Mix Ratio** — Simple 4 parts filler to 1 part hardener with epoxy thinner adjustment for optimal spray viscosity

## Usage

Mix 4 parts filler to 1 part hardener with epoxy thinner to achieve desired viscosity. Apply **2–3 coats** with flash-off between coats. Sand smooth before topcoat application. Ideal for car body repair and furniture surface preparation.
