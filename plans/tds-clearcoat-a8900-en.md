# HAIWEN A-8900 ACE High-Hardness Clear Coat

## 1. Product Overview

A-8900 Clear Coat is a high-solid, two-component acrylic polyurethane clear coat formulated for high durability and hardness. It delivers a brilliant mirror-like finish while providing enhanced scratch resistance and long-term protection, making it ideal for professional repair shops focusing on premium-quality refinishing. Suitable for various vehicle models and repair applications where hardness and longevity are priorities.

**Differentiator:** Compared with B-8800, A-8900 offers higher film hardness, better chemical and abrasion resistance, and superior long-term durability, while maintaining excellent gloss.

## 2. Key Features

- Higher hardness and scratch resistance than standard clear coats
- Three types of A-900 Hardener for temperature adaptability
- Mirror-like gloss with durable protective performance
- Excellent weathering, corrosion, and chemical resistance
- Suitable for partial repairs and full body refinishing

## 3. Recommended Matching Products

- Clear Coat: A-8900
- Hardener: A-900 Series
- Thinner:
  - C-166 Universal Thinner Series
  - A-199 High Performance Thinner Series

## 4. Suitable Substrates

- HAIWEN 1K Basecoat surfaces
- Fully cured and properly sanded paint films

## 5. Pot Life (25°C)

- Fast type: approx. 3 hours
- Normal / Medium type: approx. 4 hours

## 6. Mixing & Application

- Clear Coat : Hardener : Thinner = 100 : 50 : 20–30 (weight ratio)
- Pot Life (25°C): Fast ~3 h, Normal / Slow ~4 h
- Spray Gun: 1.2–1.4 mm nozzle, 1.8–2.5 kg/cm²
- Application: 2–3 thin, even coats; spray distance 15–20 cm; flash-off 5–10 min

### Hardeners by Temperature

| Temperature | Recommended Hardener |
|-------------|----------------------|
| < 15°C      | A-900 Fast           |
| 15–25°C     | A-900 Normal         |
| > 25°C      | A-900 Slow           |

## 7. Drying Time

| Temperature | Dust-Free Time                | Polishing Time                |
|-------------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| 25°C        | Fast: 20 min / Normal: 30 min / Slow: 40 min | Fast: 12–16 h / Normal: 16–20 h / Slow: 20–40 h |
| 60°C        | Fast: 5 min / Normal: 10 min / Slow: 15 min  | Fast: 30 min / Normal: 35 min / Slow: 40 min    |

## 8. Notes

- Stir well before use
- Avoid thick layers to maintain adhesion and gloss
- Close container after use
- Compatible with 1K / 2K topcoats
- Minor dust or runs can be wet-sanded (1200–2000 grit) after full cure
- High-hardness finish reduces susceptibility to minor scratches during long-term service

## 9. Safety Information

- For professional use only
- Keep away from heat, sparks, and children
