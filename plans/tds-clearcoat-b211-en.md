# HAIWEN B-211 High-Viscosity Clear Coat

## 1. Product Overview

B-211 Clear Coat is a high-viscosity, two-component acrylic polyurethane clear coat designed for enhanced film thickness and superior coverage. Its formulation provides excellent gloss, smooth finish, and strong protective properties, suitable for professional automotive repairs and refinishing where a thicker protective layer is desired.

**Differentiator:** Compared to standard clear coats, B-211 offers higher viscosity, resulting in better leveling and improved surface coverage, ideal for heavy-duty or premium finishes.

## 2. Key Features

- High-viscosity formulation → thicker coating in fewer passes
- Excellent mirror-like gloss and smoothness
- Suitable for partial repairs, panels, or full-body refinishing
- Strong adhesion, weathering resistance, and chemical resistance

## 3. Recommended Matching Products

- Clear Coat: B-211
- Hardener: B-800 Series
- Thinner: C-66 Series

## 4. Mixing & Application

- Mixing Ratio (Weight): Clear Coat : Hardener : Thinner = 2 : 1 : 1
- Pot Life (25°C): Fast ~3 h, Normal / Slow ~4 h
- Spray Gun: 1.2–1.4 mm nozzle, 1.8–2.5 kg/cm²
- Application: Apply 2–3 even coats, maintaining 15–20 cm spray distance; flash-off 5–10 min

## 5. Drying Time

| Temperature | Dust-Free Time | Polishing Time |
|-------------|----------------|----------------|
| 25°C        | 25–30 min      | 12–16 h        |
| 60°C        | 5–10 min       | 30–40 min      |

## 6. Notes

- Stir well before use
- Avoid thick layers to maintain adhesion and gloss
- Close container after use
- Compatible with 1K / 2K topcoats
- Minor dust or runs can be wet-sanded (1200–2000 grit) after full cure
- High-hardness finish reduces susceptibility to minor scratches during long-term service

## 7. Safety Information

- For professional use only
- Keep away from heat, sparks, and children
