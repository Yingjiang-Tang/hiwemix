# HAIWEN S-504 1K Plastic Primer

## Product Overview

HAIWEN S-504 1K Plastic Primer is a specialized adhesion primer specifically formulated for automotive plastic substrates including bumpers, trim panels, and body components. It delivers exceptional bonding between plastic and paint, creating a reliable foundation for subsequent 1K basecoat and topcoat applications.

## Key Features

- **Plastic Surface Primer** — Specifically formulated for automotive plastic parts including bumpers, trim panels, and body components
- **Enhanced Adhesion Performance** — Promotes strong bonding between plastic and paint to prevent peeling, chipping, and paint failure
- **Easy 1K Application** — Fast drying, ready-to-spray formulation with excellent adhesion promotion for professional results

## Usage

Apply **1–2 light coats** on clean, sanded plastic surfaces. Enhances adhesion between plastic and paint to prevent peeling. Top with 1K basecoat or topcoat after flash-off. Ideal for bumper painting and plastic part refinishing.
