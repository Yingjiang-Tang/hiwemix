# HAIWEN HW-L04 BPO Light-weight Putty

## 1. Product Description

An upgraded version of the yellow body filler, adopting a pure BPO (Benzoyl Peroxide) curing system. Based on the performance of mainstream body fillers, microspheres are added to reduce the product density by more than 20% compared to conventional body fillers. It focuses on significantly improving spreading and sanding properties. It features a light and smooth spreading feel, excellent sanding dust production, high sanding efficiency, and outstanding workability. This product can reduce sandpaper consumption and is suitable for a wide range of substrates.

## 2. Application Scope

Generally used before the application of primer surfacer (mid-coat) to fill and repair substrate defects such as dents, pinholes, cracks, and small welds. After curing and sanding, it provides a smooth and flat substrate surface. It is widely used in repair processes for cars, trucks, machine tools, and machinery, and is suitable for various metal substrates.

## 4. Instructions for Use

- Remove oil, rust, old paint films, and moisture from the substrate surface. Ensure the surface is completely clean and dry before spreading.
- Mix the body filler and special hardener thoroughly at the specified ratio (100:2) until the color is uniform. Complete the application within the pot life, otherwise, it will gel and become unusable.
- Spread the mixed filler onto the substrate surface to be repaired. It is recommended to rough-spread first for the base, and then thin-spread for a smooth finish to reduce pinholes. If bubbles are trapped during application, scrape them completely with a spreader; otherwise, poor adhesion such as blistering or peeling may occur.
- After complete curing, sand the putty film to the desired state according to user needs. The optimal sanding time is within 30 minutes.
- Ensure the putty film is completely dry before applying primer surfacer (mid-coat) or topcoat; otherwise, peeling or blistering of the paint film may easily occur.

## 5. Precautions

- If uneven layers occur due to prolonged storage, it is recommended to stir thoroughly with a clean steel ruler or scraper until the color is uniform from top to bottom before use. Delamination will not occur during short-term storage.
- For thick applications, it is best to apply multiple thin layers to reach the required thickness (recommended maximum thickness should not exceed 5mm).
- After use, the filler must be sealed and stored immediately to prevent solvent evaporation from thickening the filler or degrading the main product. Filler mixed with hardener must not be returned to the original can, otherwise, it will cause the main filler to degrade.
- Use the hardener as soon as possible after opening to avoid swelling of the tube, water separation, or loss of effectiveness.
- It is strictly forbidden to mix non-standard solvents like "thinner" (lacquer thinner) to dilute the body filler; otherwise, it will cause blistering and peeling of the putty film. If dilution is required, please contact the seller to purchase the specific matching resin thinner.
- This product is compatible with various primers, but it cannot be applied over phenolic primers or alkyd primers to avoid peeling and blistering. Ensure the primer is completely dry before spreading to avoid peeling.
- The product is equipped with a specific BPO hardener; generic polyester putty (atomic ash) hardeners cannot be used.
- Store in a cool, dry place, away from high temperatures and ignition sources, and avoid direct sunlight.

## 6. Storage

Shelf life: 12 months (Room temperature, away from light).
