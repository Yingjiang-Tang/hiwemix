# HAIWEN S-505 1K Plastic Primer Plus

## Product Overview

HAIWEN S-505 1K Plastic Primer Plus is a universal adhesion primer specially formulated for PP plastic, impurity plastics, and automotive plastic substrates. It delivers super adhesion between difficult plastic materials and paint, creating a reliable foundation for subsequent 1K basecoat and topcoat applications. Ideal where standard plastic primers fail.

## Key Features

- **Universal Plastic Compatibility** — Formulated for PP plastic, impurity plastics, and various automotive plastic surfaces including bumpers and trim panels
- **Super Adhesion Performance** — Provides maximum bonding strength between plastic and paint, preventing peeling and failure on challenging substrates
- **Easy 1K Application** — Fast drying, ready-to-spray formulation with enhanced adhesion promotion for professional results

## Usage

Apply **1–2 light coats** on clean, sanded PP plastic or impurity plastic surfaces. Provides super adhesion on challenging substrates like bumpers. Top with 1K basecoat or topcoat after flash-off. Ideal for bumper painting and PP plastic refinishing.
