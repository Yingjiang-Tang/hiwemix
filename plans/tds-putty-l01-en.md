# HAIWEN HW-L01 Export BPO-Polyester Putty

## 1. Product Feature

Specifically adjusted for the application metrics most valued by today's users, such as spreading, sanding, and drying speed. The product offers excellent workability, fast drying, and dry sanding without clogging the sandpaper.

## 2. Application Scope

Can be used on iron, steel, aluminum, aluminum alloys, galvanized steel, fiberglass (FRP), and primed metal surfaces.
