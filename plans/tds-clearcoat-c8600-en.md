# HAIWEN C-8600 Crystal Clearcoat

## 1. Product Overview

HAIWEN C-8600 Crystal Clearcoat is a cost-effective two-component acrylic polyurethane clear coat, designed for excellent application performance and outstanding physical and chemical properties. Formulated to deliver high gloss, strong durability, and stable weather resistance, C-8600 is widely used in automotive repair shops and authorized 4S service centers, suitable for a broad range of vehicle models and refinishing applications.

## 2. Product Features

- High cost-performance ratio, ideal for daily refinish operations
- No thinner required, simplified mixing and stable film build
- Compatible with three hardener speed options, adaptable to various working temperatures
- Excellent mirror-like gloss and depth
- Outstanding weather resistance, corrosion resistance, and abrasion resistance
- Suitable for partial or full vehicle refinishing

## 3. Matching Products

- Clear Coat: C-8600 Crystal Clearcoat
- Hardener: HAIWEN C-600 Hardener Series (Fast / Normal / Slow)

## 4. Suitable Substrates

- HAIWEN 1K Basecoat surface
- Other fully cured and properly sanded paint films

## 5. Spray Gun Setup

- Nozzle Size: 1.2 – 1.4 mm
- Spray Pressure: 1.8 – 2.5 kg/cm²

## 6. Application Method

- Apply 2–3 single wet coats
- Spray distance: 15–20 cm
- Film thickness: 40–60 μm
- Flash-off time between coats: 5–10 minutes @ 25°C
- If force drying is required, allow the coated surface to stand for 15–20 minutes before baking.

## 7. Mixing Ratio

- Clearcoat : Hardener = 2 : 1
- (No thinner required)
- Mixing Ratio: by weight

## 8. Hardener Selection by Temperature

| Ambient Temperature | Recommended Hardener |
|---------------------|----------------------|
| <15°C               | C-600 Fast           |
| 15–25°C             | C-600 Normal         |
| >25°C               | C-600 Slow           |

## 9. Pot Life (25°C)

- C-600 Fast: approx. 3 hours
- C-600 Normal / Slow: approx. 4 hours

## 10. Drying Time

| Temperature | Dust-Free Time                | Polishing Time                |
|-------------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| 25°C        | Fast: 20 min / Normal: 30 min / Slow: 40 min | Fast: 12–16 h / Normal: 16–20 h / Slow: 20–40 h |
| 60°C        | Fast: 5 min / Normal: 10 min / Slow: 15 min  | Fast: 30 min / Normal: 35 min / Slow: 40 min    |

## 11. Notes

- When ambient temperature is below 15°C, air drying is not recommended; baking is advised.
- When temperature exceeds 30°C, adding an appropriate amount of anti-whitening agent is recommended (refer to product instructions).
- Clean all spray equipment immediately after use with suitable thinner to prevent curing inside the tools.
- Mixed clearcoat must be used within pot life and cannot be stored.
- Minor defects such as dust nibs or runs can be wet-sanded with 1200#–2000# grit after full curing, followed by polishing.

## 12. Safety Information

- Wear appropriate personal protective equipment during application
- Keep away from heat, open flames, and children
- For further information, please contact the supplier
