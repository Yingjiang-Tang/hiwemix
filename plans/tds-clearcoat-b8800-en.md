# HAIWEN B-8800 Diamond Clear Coat

## 1. Product Overview

B-8800 Diamond Clear Coat is a high-solid, two-component acrylic polyurethane clear coat featuring excellent application performance and outstanding physical and chemical properties. It delivers a brilliant high-gloss finish with excellent cost performance, making it widely used in professional automotive repair shops. The product is suitable for a wide range of vehicle models and repair applications.

## 2. Product Features

- Available with three types of hardeners and thinners, adaptable to different temperatures and working environments
- Exceptional mirror-like gloss, enhancing the vehicle's premium appearance
- Formulated with high-quality isocyanate hardeners for excellent weather resistance, corrosion resistance, and abrasion resistance
- Suitable for partial repairs and full body refinishing

## 3. Recommended Matching Products

- Clear Coat: B-8800 Diamond Clear Coat
- Hardener: B-800 Series
- Thinner: C-166 Universal Thinner Series

## 4. Suitable Substrates

- HAIWEN 1K Basecoat surfaces
- Other fully cured and properly sanded paint films

## 5. Mixing Ratio & Technical Data

- Clear Coat : Hardener : Thinner = 100 : 50 : 20–30
- Mixing Ratio: by weight

### Hardeners Selection by Temperature

| Temperature | Recommended Hardener     |
|-------------|--------------------------|
| Below 15°C  | Fast hardener recommended |
| 15°C–25°C   | Standard hardener recommended |
| Above 25°C  | Slow hardener recommended |

## 6. Pot Life (25°C)

- Fast type: approx. 3 hours
- Normal / Medium type: approx. 4 hours

## 7. Application Method

- Apply 2–3 single wet coats
- Spray distance: 15–20 cm
- Recommended film thickness: 40–60 μm
- Flash-off time between coats: 5–10 minutes (25°C)
- If baking is required, allow 15–20 minutes flash-off before baking

## 8. Spray Gun Setup

- Nozzle size: 1.2 – 1.4 mm
- Spray pressure: 1.8 – 2.5 kg/cm²

## 9. Drying Time

| Temperature        | Dust-Free Time                | Polishing Time                |
|--------------------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Air Dry @ 25°C     | Fast: 20 min / Normal: 30 min / Slow: 40 min | Fast: 12–16 h / Normal: 16–20 h / Slow: 20–40 h |
| Force Dry @ 60°C   | Fast: 5 min / Normal: 10 min / Slow: 15 min  | Fast: 30 min / Normal: 35 min / Slow: 40 min    |

## 10. Notes

- When ambient temperature is below 15°C, air drying is not recommended; baking is advised.
- When temperature exceeds 30°C, adding an appropriate amount of anti-whitening agent is recommended (refer to product instructions).
- Clean all spray equipment immediately after use with suitable thinner to prevent curing inside the tools.
- Mixed clearcoat must be used within pot life and cannot be stored.
- Minor defects such as dust nibs or runs can be wet-sanded with 1200#–2000# grit after full curing, followed by polishing.

## 11. Safety Information

- For professional use only
- Wear appropriate personal protective equipment during application
- Keep away from heat, open flames, and children
- For further information, please contact the supplier
