# HAIWEN System 1K Basecoat Series

## Product Overview

HAIWEN 1K Basecoat Series is a **high-quality one-component basecoat system**, including solid colors, metallic colors, and pearl colors.

It delivers excellent metallic brilliance and depth, enhancing the **three-dimensional appearance** of the vehicle surface and presenting a premium, refined finish.

The system allows **flexible color combinations** and **precise color adjustment**, fully meeting the requirements of automotive refinishing and color matching.

## Product Composition

- **Basecoat:** HAIWEN 1K Color Master-batch (1K Basecoat Colors)
- **Thinner:**
  - HAIWEN Universal Thinner (C-166)
  - HAIWEN High Performance Thinner (B-188)

## Suitable Substrates

- Existing paint film
- 2K primer surface sanded with P600–P1000 sandpaper

## Surface Preparation

- Ensure the surface is clean, dry, and free from oil, dust, and contaminants
- Wipe thoroughly using a dust cloth before application

## Pot Life (25°C)

- 24 hours at 25°C
- If the mixed material is left unused for more than 1 hour, stir thoroughly before application

## Mixing Ratio (by weight)

**1K Basecoat : Thinner = 1 : 0.8 – 1.0**

## Spray Gun Setup

- **Nozzle size:** 1.2 – 1.4 mm
- **Spray pressure:** 1.8 – 2.5 kg/cm²

## Application Method

1. Apply **2–3 single coats** at a spray pressure of **1.8–2.5 kg/cm²**
2. **Flash-off time:** 2–3 minutes at 20°C between coats
3. **Spray distance:** 15–20 cm
4. After full color coverage is achieved, apply the final control coat at approximately **2.0 kg/cm²**
5. Allow the basecoat to flash off for **2–10 minutes**, then apply clear coat **within 1 hour**

## Drying Time

- 15 minutes at 25°C

## Important Notes

- After spraying HAIWEN 1K metallic or pearl basecoat, apply clear coat as soon as possible and within 1 hour once the basecoat is fully dry
- To ensure color accuracy and formula repeatability, **stir thoroughly before use**

## Packing Specification

- 1 L / Tin
- 4 L / Tin

## Storage

- Store in a cool, dry, and well-ventilated place
- **Shelf life:** 24 months in unopened original containers under normal storage conditions
