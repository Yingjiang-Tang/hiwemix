# HAIWEN S-506 1K PP Plastic White Primer

## Product Overview

HAIWEN S-506 1K PP Plastic White Primer is a specialized white primer formulated for PP plastic substrates including bumpers, trim panels, and automotive components. It delivers strong bonding on polypropylene surfaces while providing excellent hiding power for light-colored topcoats, with rapid drying for efficient workshop workflow.

## Key Features

- **White PP Plastic Primer** — Specifically formulated for PP plastic substrates including bumpers, trim panels, and automotive components
- **Excellent Adhesion & Fast Drying** — Provides strong bonding on polypropylene surfaces with rapid drying for efficient workshop workflow
- **Easy Sanding Application** — Smooth sandability for effortless surface preparation, ready for 1K basecoat or topcoat application

## Usage

Apply **1–2 light coats** on clean, sanded PP plastic surfaces. Fast drying with 10–15 minutes flash-off. Easy to sand for smooth preparation. Top with 1K basecoat or topcoat. Ideal for bumper painting and PP plastic refinishing.
