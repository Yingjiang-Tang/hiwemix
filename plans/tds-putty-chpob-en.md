# HAIWEN CHPO-Poly Putty — B Instructions (Premium-Grade White Flowable)

## 1. Product Description

A premium-grade white flowable unsaturated polyester filler for export. Building upon the performance advantages of mainstream flowable export products, this filler features enhanced sandability and improved application feel, along with faster drying speed, delivering outstanding all-around performance.

## 2. Application Range

Generally applied before primer surfacer, this product is used to fill and smooth out substrate defects such as dents, pinholes, shrinkage marks, cracks, and small weld seams. After curing and sanding, the substrate surface becomes level and smooth. Widely used in automotive, truck, machine tool, and machinery repair, as well as furniture and renovation applications.

## 4. Directions for Use

1. **Surface Preparation:** Remove all oil, grease, rust, old paint, and moisture from the substrate surface. Ensure the surface is clean and completely dry before application.
2. **Mixing:** Thoroughly mix the body filler with hardener at the specified ratio (100:2 by weight) until the color is uniform. Apply within the pot life; otherwise, the mixture will gel and become unusable.
3. **Application:** Apply the mixed filler onto the damaged substrate surface. It is recommended to first apply a rough base coat, then apply a thin finishing coat to minimize sand craters. If air bubbles become trapped during application, scrape them out completely with a putty knife; failure to do so may result in reduced adhesion.
4. **Sanding:** After the filler has fully cured, sand the filler film to the desired finish per the user's requirements.
5. **Topcoating:** Before applying primer surfacer or topcoat, ensure the filler film is completely dry (especially after wet sanding), otherwise the paint film may peel or blister.

## 5. Precautions

- The filler may separate during storage or transit. Stir thoroughly with a clean steel ruler or spatula until the color is uniform before use.
- For thick applications, build up multiple thin coats to the desired thickness (maximum recommended thickness: 5 mm per coat).
- Seal the container immediately after use to prevent solvent evaporation, which may cause the filler to thicken or deteriorate. Do not return filler contaminated with hardener to the original container, as this will spoil the unused filler.
- Use the hardener promptly after opening to avoid tube swelling, moisture formation, or loss of activity.
- Do not add unauthorized solvents such as lacquer thinner to thin the filler, as this will cause blistering and peeling of the filler film. If thinning is required, contact the supplier for the recommended proprietary resin thinner.
- This product is compatible with most primers, but must not be applied over phenolic primers or alkyd primers, as this may cause peeling and blistering. Ensure the primer is fully dry before application.
- Do not apply directly onto galvanized steel surfaces.
- Store in a cool, dry place, away from heat, open flames, and direct sunlight.
- **This product is for export only.**

## 6. Storage

Shelf life: 9 months (cool, dry, away from light).
