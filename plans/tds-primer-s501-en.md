# HAIWEN S-501 1K Grey Primer Plus

## Product Overview

HAIWEN S-501 1K Grey Primer Plus is a single-component grey primer formulated for PP/ABS plastic parts. Made from **chlorinated PP-modified acrylic resin**, it offers fast drying, excellent filling, and a smooth film that levels sanding marks and minor dents, providing a reliable foundation for subsequent topcoats.

With an improved formulation that outperforms S-502, S-501 delivers superior surface preparation and enhanced sealing for professional car refinishing.

## Key Features

- **Enhanced Grey Primer Formula** — Improved formulation that performs better than S-502 for superior surface preparation
- **Premium Adhesion & Sealing** — Provides excellent paint adhesion and enhanced surface sealing for professional car refinishing
- **Easy 1K Application** — Fast drying with excellent sandability, ready-to-spray formulation for efficient workshop use

## Usage

Apply **1–2 coats** on clean, sanded surfaces. Sand lightly before applying basecoat or topcoat. Ideal for auto body repair and premium painting preparation.
