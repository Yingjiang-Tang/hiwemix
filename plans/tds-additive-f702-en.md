# HAIWEN F-702 Degreaser

## 1. Application & Features

Formulated for cleaning oil, grease, and contaminants from the surface of substrates prior to spraying. This product is a specially modified structural compound delivering outstanding degreasing and surface-cleaning efficiency.

## 2. Usage

- Apply to substrate surface before priming or topcoat spraying
- Wipe clean with lint-free cloth; allow surface to fully evaporate before paint application
- For heavily contaminated surfaces, repeat the cleaning process

## 3. Notes

- Pre-paint surface preparation only — not a paint thinner
- Test on sensitive substrates first
- Shelf life and storage: refer to standard HAIWEN additive storage conditions (cool, dry, well-ventilated place)
