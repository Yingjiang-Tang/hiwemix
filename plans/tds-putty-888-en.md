# HAIWEN HW-888 BPO-Fiber Putty

## 1. Product Feature

Contains internal glass fibers, providing excellent filling properties. Specially designed for large-area applications, thick coating, welds, and other highly demanding areas as a base filling. Due to the internal glass fibers, the cured filler exhibits good flexibility and does not shrink. In terms of application, compared to ordinary fiberglass fillers, it spreads more smoothly without pulling fibers (no fiber rolling).

## 2. Application Scope

Can be used on iron, steel, aluminum, aluminum alloys, galvanized steel, fiberglass (FRP), and primed metal surfaces.
