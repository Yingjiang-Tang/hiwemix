# HAIWEN F-703 Drying Accelerator

## 1. Application & Features

A chemical additive designed to speed up the paint drying process by accelerating the curing reaction. Commonly used in winter or low-temperature conditions, it is added to 2K paints to enhance curing speed. Ensure thorough mixing after adding it to the paint in the specified ratio.

## 2. Dosage

Typically, the addition amount ranges from 1% to 5% of the total coating weight. The optimal ratio should be determined based on the application environment or via empirical testing to ensure fast drying while enhancing coating adhesion and durability. **Note:** Excessive addition may cause the paint film to become brittle or lose gloss.

## 3. Notes

- For 2K paints
- Adjust dosage based on ambient temperature; lower temperatures require higher dosage
- Mix thoroughly into the paint system before application
- Shelf life and storage: refer to standard HAIWEN additive storage conditions (cool, dry, well-ventilated place)
