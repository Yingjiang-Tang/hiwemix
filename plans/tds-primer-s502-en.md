# HAIWEN S-502 1K Grey Primer

## Product Overview

HAIWEN S-502 1K Grey Primer is a specialized automotive primer designed for surface preparation before painting. It provides excellent adhesion and sealing for car refinishing, creating the ideal foundation for subsequent 1K basecoat and topcoat applications.

## Key Features

- **Grey Automotive Primer** — Provides excellent surface sealing and adhesion for car refinishing projects
- **Smooth Surface Preparation** — Creates a uniform base layer for 1K basecoat and topcoat application with minimal sanding
- **Easy 1K Application** — Fast drying, ready-to-spray formulation with excellent sandability between coats

## Usage

Apply **1–2 coats** on clean, sanded surfaces. Provides excellent adhesion and sealing for car refinishing. Sand lightly before applying basecoat or topcoat. Ideal for auto body repair and painting preparation.
