# HAIWEN HW-L03 Export BPO-Polyester Putty — Universal Body Filler

## 1. Product Feature

High comprehensive performance. It not only addresses the practical application needs of users but also ensures highly comprehensive internal product metrics, making it a truly universal body filler.

## 2. Application Scope

Can be used on iron, steel, aluminum, aluminum alloys, galvanized steel, fiberglass (FRP), and primed metal surfaces.
