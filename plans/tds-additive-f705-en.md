# HAIWEN F-705 Matting Agent

## 1. Application & Features

Added to 2K topcoats to reduce surface gloss and achieve the desired matte effect. It offers excellent flattening performance, producing a smooth, fine, and delicate paint film with high versatility across various applications.

## 2. Dosage

Determine the addition amount based on the required gloss level. Different mixing ratios yield distinct gloss levels.

## 3. Notes

- For 2K topcoats only
- Adjust dosage based on target gloss level (semi-gloss, matte, deep matte, etc.)
- Mix thoroughly into the paint system before application
- Shelf life and storage: refer to standard HAIWEN additive storage conditions (cool, dry, well-ventilated place)
