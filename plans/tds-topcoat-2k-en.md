# HAIWEN System 2K Topcoat Series

## Product Overview

HAIWEN System 2K Topcoat Series is a high-performance two-component automotive topcoat based on advanced **acrylic polyurethane technology**.

When combined with **HDI trimer hardener**, the coating system delivers excellent **weather resistance**, **mechanical strength**, and **chemical durability**.

The product adopts a **single-stage application process**, making it ideal for **medium- and high-grade automotive refinishing and repair**, providing outstanding gloss, color stability, and long-term protection.

## Products & Additives

- **Topcoat:** HAIWEN 2K Solid Color Topcoat
- **Hardener:** HAIWEN C-600 Series
- **Thinner:** HAIWEN C-166 Series

## Suitable Substrates

- Old paint film
- Primer surface sanded with P600–P1000 grit sandpaper

## Surface Preparation

- Clean the surface thoroughly using a dust cloth
- Ensure the surface is clean, dry, and free from oil or contaminants

## Mixing Ratio (by weight)

**2K Topcoat : Hardener : Thinner = 2 : 1 : 1**

## Hardener & Thinner Selection (by Temperature)

| Temperature | Hardener | Thinner |
|---|---|---|
| <15 °C | C-600 Fast | C-166 Fast |
| 15–25 °C | C-600 Normal | C-166 Normal |
| >25 °C | C-600 Slow | C-166 Slow |

## Pot Life (25°C)

- **C-600 Fast:** approx. 3 hours
- **C-600 Normal:** approx. 4 hours

## Spray Gun Setup

- **Nozzle:** 1.2 – 1.4 mm
- **Spray Pressure:** 1.8 – 2.5 kg/cm²

## Application Method

- Apply **2–3 single wet coats**
- **Spray distance:** 15–20 cm
- **Film thickness:** 40–60 μm
- **Flash-off time between coats:** 5–10 minutes @ 25°C
- After spraying, allow the surface to stand for 15–20 minutes before force drying

## Drying Time

### Air Dry @ 25°C

| Hardener | Dust-Free Time | Polishing Interval |
|---|---|---|
| C-600 Fast | 30 min | 15 hrs |
| C-600 Normal | 40 min | 20 hrs |
| C-600 Slow | 50 min | 50 hrs |

### Force Dry @ 60°C

| Hardener | Dust-Free Time | Polishing Interval |
|---|---|---|
| C-600 Fast | 5 min | 30 min |
| C-600 Normal | 10 min | 40 min |
| C-600 Slow | 15 min | 50 min |

## Notes

- When spraying over thermoplastic paint surfaces, apply 2–3 single wet coats with 5–10 minutes flash-off between coats
- Stir thoroughly before use to ensure color accuracy and repeatability
- When spraying on plastic parts, apply plastic primer first; a flexible additive may be added if required
- For polishing or repair, ensure the coating film is fully cured before proceeding

## Safety Information

- For professional use only
- Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Keep away from heat, sparks, and open flames
- Keep out of reach of children
- Refer to SDS for detailed safety information

## Packing Specification

## Storage

- Store in a cool, dry, and well-ventilated place
- **Shelf life:** 24 months in unopened original packaging
