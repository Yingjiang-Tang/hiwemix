# HAIWEN S-509 2K White Epoxy Primer

## Product Overview

HAIWEN S-509 2K White Epoxy Primer is a professional-grade corrosion protection primer specifically formulated for automotive metal substrates. This high-performance white epoxy primer creates a superior protective barrier against rust and corrosion on steel, aluminum, and iron surfaces, providing excellent adhesion and long-lasting protection as the ideal foundation for subsequent topcoat applications.

Models with the same specifications but different colors: **S-510** (2K Grey Epoxy Primer); **S-512** (2K Zinc Yellow Epoxy Primer).

## Key Features

- **White Epoxy Primer** — Haiwen quality 2K epoxy primer specifically formulated for automotive metal surface protection
- **Superior Anti-Corrosion** — Creates a protective barrier against rust and corrosion on steel, aluminum, and iron substrates
- **Excellent Adhesion** — Provides strong bonding to metal surfaces with long-lasting durability for reliable protection before topcoating

## Usage

Mix with epoxy hardener (**H-282**) and epoxy thinner at **4 : 1 : 2** ratio. Apply **2–3 coats** on clean, sanded metal surfaces. Provides superior anti-corrosion barrier against rust on steel, aluminum, and iron. Ideal for auto body panels, chassis, and metal parts before topcoating.
